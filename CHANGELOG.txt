StatsPage changelog
===================

1.0   Release. Four more tabs in the Archives screen of the in-game menu, behind Collectibles, Enemies and
      Tutorials: Completion, Story, Zones and Dylan. One completion bar and a count for each kind of thing
      the game lets you do or find, read from your latest save each time the Archives open. Counts per zone,
      the names of what is still missing and where it comes from, your place on each SHUM machine, and a row
      for each side story that comes in parts. The pages hold back what would spoil the story (names of
      story things not done yet, story totals, zones not visited, names of locked items); each of these is
      a setting in statscompletiontracker_config.ini or under Options > MODS (Mod Settings Menu). The mod
      changes no game code and no save. Made for game version 1.4.1.
